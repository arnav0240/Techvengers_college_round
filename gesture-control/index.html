How to drive it

  1. 🧭 Calibrate: look straight ahead, hold still, press Calibrate neutral.
  2. 🗺️ Steer: lean out of the circle around your neutral nose — the crosshair moves faster the farther you go. Drift back inside to stop.
  3. 🤏 Pinch thumb + index → CLICK. Release to re-arm the next click.
  4. 🖐️ Open palm, hold still → PAUSED (any gesture resumes).
  5. ☝️ Point up / down → SCROLL UP / DOWN (scroll mode).
  6. ✊ Fist, hold briefly → CURSOR mode badge.
  7. ✌️ Two fingers, hold 0.7s → SCROLL mode badge.
